# Source artwork and visual implementation

Original supplied PNG: 1448 × 1086 pixels; SHA-256 `49a5ce45452f483ff0e37160c0a98ffd74d5244bf2709a939dd9b6eb575e5c4c`.

`reference/homepage-final.png` and `public/artwork/homepage-final.png` are unchanged copies of the user-provided artwork. `components/Artwork.tsx` uses SVG clip paths and a color-key filter to isolate the original tree, swing, bird, wordmark, and irregular paper boards. Input areas and obsolete footer wording are excluded from the decorative composition. Native input, textarea, button, and links supply real interaction and accessible names.

The exact illustrated brand lettering and tagline are source pixels. Editable labels and field text use the self-hosted Annie Use Your Telescope font, distributed under the SIL Open Font License included beside the font. This approximates the artwork's handwriting; it is not claimed to be its original font. Orange texture is approximated in CSS; source ink and paper detail remain intact.

Desktop preserves the tree/swing on the left and dangling form on the right. Additional recipient rows expand the board and scene. On phones the original decorative tree and bird recompose around a stacked form; controls remain legible and do not require sideways scrolling. The obsolete “read a message” footer option is omitted.

Bird flight and an envelope appear after confirmed request processing only. Reduced-motion preference disables the movement. No flying bird or sent confirmation appears in the disabled release.
